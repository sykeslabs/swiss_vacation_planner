# ADAM — Adaptive Days-off Allocation Manager

*Elevator pitch · about 1 minute · about 150 words*

---

In AI, we all share a common friend: **Adam**, the Adaptive Moment Estimation optimizer. He tunes our parameters, minimizes our loss, and hopefully makes our models a little smarter.

Today I want to introduce you to a different ADAM: the **Adaptive Days-off Allocation Manager.**

Instead of optimizing neural networks, ADAM optimizes your holidays. It answers one very important question:

> **How do you get the most time off with the fewest vacation days?**

That sounds simple, but in Switzerland it isn't. Every canton has different public holidays. Some holidays fall on weekends, not everyone works Monday to Friday, and everyone has their own constraints. So the real challenge wasn't building the optimizer. It was **defining the problem correctly.**

My prototype takes your **work location**, your **working days** and your **vacation budget**. It then calculates exactly when to take your days off to get the longest breaks, shows **historical weather** for those dates, and suggests **travel ideas** for where to go.

The original Adam optimizes the model…
**ADAM optimizes your life.**

After all the time we've spent teaching machines…
it's about time we optimized something that really matters.
